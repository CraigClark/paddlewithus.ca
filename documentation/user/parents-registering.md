# How parents register

## Swim test (under 15)

1. Parent goes to `/program/swim-test`.
2. Sessions list shows only available (non-full, future) slots.
3. Click **Register for this swim test** on a slot.
4. The form lists the parent's eligible family members (under 15, not already cleared):
   - Kids who already passed, attested, or are exempt appear disabled.
   - Kids already registered for a future swim test are disabled with a note about their existing booking.
5. Check one or more participants.
6. Click **Register**.
7. No cart, no checkout. Registration is immediate and free.
8. Two confirmation emails are sent: one to the parent, one to PICC staff.

## Swim attestation (15+)

1. Parent goes to the swim attestation page (path managed in content).
2. The form lists eligible family members (15+, not already cleared).
3. Check one or more participants.
4. Check the attestation checkbox: *"I confirm that the participants selected above are able to roll into deep water, tread water for 1 minute, and swim 50 metres unassisted."*
5. Click **Submit attestation**.
6. Profile swim status is set to "Attestation on file". No commerce order is created.
7. Two confirmation emails are sent: one to the parent, one to PICC staff.

## Cancelling a registration

Parents cannot self-cancel. To cancel:
- Email `petriecanoe@gmail.com`
- The commerce manager cancels the order in the admin UI. This restores the slot capacity automatically.
